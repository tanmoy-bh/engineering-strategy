How it was measured

Sources: Apple's public review feed for the Singapore App Store and Google Play's public reviews for Singapore, each giving the latest 500 written reviews, newest first. Sentiment: 4 to 5 stars positive, 3 neutral, 1 to 2 negative, cross-checked with a list of 124 positive and negative words with negation handling. Themes: keyword rules; 56 App Store and 104 Google Play reviews matched no theme. Recent figures rest on 42 App Store and 100 Google Play reviews, so read them as direction, not precision. Only aggregates and paraphrased themes are stored on this page.

04 · Guarded release

Every app release guards unlock success, not just crashes

Version 4.12 rolls out in stages. A stage advances only if unlock success stays within the guardrail. Try it.

A stage needs at least 300 unlocks before it is judged, so small cohorts don't raise false alarms. Sample data.

05 · AI copilot

AI drafts the incident, a person decides

An LLM judge scores every draft first. Nothing reaches customers automatically.

Why I built this

At PropertyGuru I run the same loop: Datadog, Kibana and Sentry for visibility, Squadcast for on-call, and AI with human review. A custom MCP server made our bug fixes about 30% faster. A shared UI kit took deploys from once every 2 weeks to 2–3 a week.
